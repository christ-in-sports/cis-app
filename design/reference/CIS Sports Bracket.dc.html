D · Options explored for a phone-width bracket

9:41
Sports
Bracket options
1 · Rounds stacked (chosen)
Every game readable at full size. Round jump chips replace scrolling. "Winner plays …" text does the job of connector lines.
2 · Swipe between rounds
One round at a time, so you cannot see where a team goes next. Horizontal swipes also fight the PWA's own back gesture.
3 · Connector lines
About 480px wide for 8 teams, so it pans sideways with text at roughly half size. Placement games have no natural place in the tree.
